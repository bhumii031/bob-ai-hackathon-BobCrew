# Problem Statement

## Who Experiences This Problem?

- **Pharmacovigilance scientists** at pharmaceutical companies and regulatory agencies
- **Regulatory affairs managers** preparing NDA / MAA / IMPD submissions
- **Drug safety officers** responsible for periodic safety update reports (PSURs)

## The Problem

### Signal Detection
Pharmacovigilance teams must manually analyse thousands of spontaneous adverse event (AE) reports to identify potential drug safety signals. This requires:
- Specialist statistical knowledge (PRR, ROR, disproportionality analysis)
- Access to expensive licensed software (Oracle Argus, VigiBase, etc.)
- Days to weeks of analysis time per dataset

Delayed or missed signals can result in patient harm, regulatory enforcement action, and product recalls.

### Regulatory Submission Readiness
Preparing a regulatory dossier to the ICH M4 Common Technical Document (CTD) standard requires cross-referencing hundreds of documents against a complex 5-module reference structure. Manual gap analysis:
- Is error-prone and time-consuming
- Requires deep regulatory expertise
- Often surfaces critical gaps only after submission — triggering Major Objections (MO) or Other Concerns (OC) from authorities, costing months of delay

## Impact
- Average cost of a failed regulatory submission: **$300M–$800M** in delayed market access
- Average time lost to signal detection backlogs: **6–18 months**
- Small biotech and generic manufacturers lack access to expensive PV software tools
